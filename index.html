Built around the wallet

The iOS app stays the source of truth for the user.

Walker starts with the person, not the partner. The app collects movement, shows wallet status, manages connected partners, and will own the habit rules that shape screen access.

No financial framing. Credits are designed for incentives and habit loops, not cash value.
