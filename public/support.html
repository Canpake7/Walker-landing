Walker Support

Use this page for account, privacy, HealthKit, Screen Time, and partner-connection help.

Before App Store submission, add the monitored Walker support email or helpdesk URL here. Do not submit with an unmonitored contact path.

Delete Your Account

Open Walker on iOS, go to Account settings, then choose Delete account. Walker confirms your intent, deletes your server account data, and clears local Walker data after the deletion succeeds.

Apple Health Permissions

Walker reads step count and active energy only after you grant Apple Health permission. To change access, open iOS Settings, choose Health, then manage Walker's permissions.

Screen Time Permissions

Screen Time app choices stay on your device. You can turn off Screen timer in Walker Account settings. You can also manage Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings.

Partner Connections

Open Account settings, choose Connected partners, then disconnect any partner you no longer want to access your Walker wallet. Spending access can be turned off without deleting your Walker account.
