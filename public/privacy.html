Privacy

Walker Privacy Policy

Effective June 5, 2026. Walker turns movement into wellness credits: 1 step = 1 credit. Credits are wellness incentives, not money, stored value, crypto, or transferable balance.

Walker collects only the data needed to sign you in, sync eligible movement, maintain your credit wallet, manage approved partner connections, and support local app access controls.

Account And Sign-In Data

Walker stores your email address, internal Walker player ID, sign-in provider, provider subject ID, optional display name, optional avatar URL, account timestamps, and hashed Walker session tokens. Google or Apple may separately process sign-in data under their own policies.

Health And Fitness Data

After you grant Apple Health permission, Walker reads step count and active energy. Walker uploads daily aggregates with local activity date, start/end timestamps, source, steps, and active calories. Walker does not collect routes, location, heart rate, workouts, detailed raw samples, or full HealthKit history.

Wallet Data

Walker stores wallet transactions linked to your account, including amount, type, reason, external reference, metadata, timestamps, and partner references when applicable. Wallet transactions are the source of truth for credit balance and transaction history.

Screen Time Data

Selected Screen Time apps stay on your device in Walker's app group storage and are not uploaded to Walker. Walker may upload local app-access deductions as wallet spend transactions with seconds, credits, occurred time, local date, time zone, source, and metering version.

Partner Connections

When you approve a partner connection, Walker stores the partner app, partner external user ID, granted scopes, connection timestamps, and hashed connection token. Partners can read wallet data only after approval and can spend credits only if you grant spending access.

Operational Data

Walker keeps operational logs and partner request audit events to run the service, detect failures, and prevent abuse. When you delete your account, retained partner audit rows are scrubbed of user-identifying fields.

Tracking

Walker does not use collected data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Deletion

You can delete your Walker account from Account settings in the iOS app. Deletion removes your Walker account and player-owned server data, then clears local auth, wallet, HealthKit upload markers, and Screen Time state from the device after the server deletion succeeds. Apple Health permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.

Contact

Before App Store submission, add the monitored Walker support contact here and in App Store Connect. Until then, use the in-app Account screen for deletion and privacy controls.
