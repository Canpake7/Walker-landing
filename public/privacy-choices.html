Privacy Controls

Walker Privacy Choices

These are the main controls available to Walker users.

Delete Walker Account And Data

In the iOS app, open Account settings and choose Delete account. This removes your Walker account and associated Walker server data, then clears local Walker data on the device after the server confirms deletion.

Disconnect Partners

In Account settings, open Connected partners and disconnect partners you no longer use. Disconnecting a partner invalidates that partner connection without deleting your Walker account.

Turn Off Screen Timer

In Account settings, turn off Screen timer. Selected app tokens are stored locally and are not uploaded to Walker.

Manage Apple Health Access

Use iOS Settings to change Apple Health access for Walker. Walker cannot revoke Apple Health permissions programmatically.

Sign Out

Sign out removes the current session from Walker on this device. Sign out does not delete your Walker account or server data.

Credits are wellness incentives only. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, converted, traded, or redeemed as money.
